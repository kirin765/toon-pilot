To the general leaving for the battlefield, the king gave a strange order.
It was to not fight unconditionally, but to judge the situation and act accordingly.
This king was Gwanghaegun, an illegitimate son who was denied investiture 5 times.
He practiced tightrope diplomacy, provoking neither the Ming dynasty nor the Later Jin.
But the sin of locking the Queen Dowager in the palace and killing his brother remained.
Eventually deposed by a coup, he spent the rest of his life on Jeju Island before passing away.
Was he an incompetent tyrant, or a strategist ahead of his time?
