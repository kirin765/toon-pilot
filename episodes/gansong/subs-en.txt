When offered a national treasure for just 1,000 won, someone paid 10 times the asking price.
In 1940, Andong, a book explaining the principles behind the creation of Hangul came out into the world.
At a time when the Japanese empire was suppressing our language and writing, the risk of losing this book too was high.
Joseon's great millionaire, Jeon Hyeong-pil, handed over 10,000 won, saying a treasure must be paid its true worth.
When the war broke out, he put this book in a paulownia wood box and protected it by using it as a pillow.
The book that almost vanished for 1,000 won is now a priceless national treasure.
