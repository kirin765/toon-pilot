The genius acknowledged by King Sejong turned his back on government office his entire life.
He wrote poetry at age 5, earning the name Ose (Five-year-old), and even King Sejong was amazed.
But at 21, when King Sejo usurped his nephew's throne, he wailed for 3 days.
Kim Si-seup burned all his books, shaved his head, and became a Buddhist monk.
The story he wrote while wandering at Namsan in Gyeongju is Korea's first novel in Classical Chinese.
Until he closed his eyes at Muryangsa Temple in Buyeo at age 59, he never took a government post.
