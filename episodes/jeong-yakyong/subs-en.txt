The king's most cherished genius became a criminal overnight.
Jeong Yak-yong, favored by King Jeongjo, was a talent who even helped build Suwon Hwaseong Fortress by inventing the Geojunggi (crane).
However, when King Jeongjo passed away, he got caught up in the Catholic persecution and was exiled to far-off Gangjin.
That exile lasted for a staggering 18 years.
But instead of despairing, he picked up his brush.
The books he wrote over 18 years at Dasanchodang (Dasan's cottage) numbered over 500, including the Mongminsimseo (Admonitions on Governing the People).
At the place where his life seemed over, Joseon's greatest scholarship was born.
