The greatest scientist of Joseon was a slave.
Born a government slave in Dongnae-hyeon, Jang Yeong-sil's skills reached the ears of the king.
King Sejong erased his social status and called him into the palace.
In 1434, the Jagyeokru, an automatic water clock that strikes a bell to tell time.
Once a slave, he rose to Daehogun (Deputy Commander), a senior 3rd rank.
But when the king's palanquin broke, he received 80 lashes, was dismissed, and vanished just like that.
