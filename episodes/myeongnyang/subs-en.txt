Myeongnyang, where 13 ships defeated 133. But for a while, it was just 1 ship.
It was right after the Joseon navy was completely wiped out in the previous naval battle.
At the narrow Uldolmok strait, the Nanjung Ilgi (War Diary) says: "Looking back at the ships of the other commanders, they had retreated to the open sea, merely watching."
While the 12 ships watched, the single flagship fired its cannons alone.
Yi Sun-sin raised the command flag to summon the commanders: "Do you want to die by martial law?"
Only then did the ships charge in, and with the currents turning, 31 enemy ships were destroyed.
Yi Sun-sin recorded that victory in just 4 words: "This was truly heaven's fortune."
Then, noting the tide had ebbed, he moved the camp, and that was the end of the diary entry.
