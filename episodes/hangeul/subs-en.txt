Joseon's greatest invention appears in the Annals without warning, in just a single sentence.
"This month, the King personally created 28 letters of Eonmun." There isn't a single record of the preparation process before that.
You think the Jiphyeonjeon (Hall of Worthies) made it for him? 2 months later, Choe Man-ri, the Deputy Head of Jiphyeonjeon, submitted a petition against it.
His argument was that making letters different from China's would turn them into barbarians themselves.
King Sejong retorted: "Do you know phonology? How many consonants and vowels are there?"
And so, Hangul became the world's only writing system with surviving records of its creator, timeline, and a book explaining its principles.
